About the book

Funny.
Heartbreaking.
True.

Living with endometriosis means spending years fighting for someone — anyone — to believe you. It means becoming fluent in a pain language that has no good translation, and learning to perform wellness you don't feel.

This memoir traces that journey: the dismissals, the diagnoses, the dark humor that kept things survivable. And then the surgery. And then the part where everything went very, very wrong.

It's a book about bodies and bureaucracy, grief and gallows humor, and what it means to rebuild a life on the other side of almost losing it.
